WINDOWS STARTUP
1. Open MetaTrader 5 and keep the account connected.
2. Complete local .env and BRIDGE_TOKEN setup from WINDOWS_QUICK_START.md.
3. Double-click START_ALL.bat.
4. Open http://localhost:3000/ only.
5. Use 127.0.0.1:8000 only for private bridge checks.
6. Do not use 127.0.0.1:8001/EA-MT5/.
7. Use cloudflared to expose only 127.0.0.1:8000 over HTTPS.
8. Configure MT5_BRIDGE_HTTP_URL and BRIDGE_TOKEN privately on the public dashboard server.

LIMITATIONS
- The cloud workspace is Linux and cannot verify the Windows-only MetaTrader5 package or the user's active desktop terminal.
- The real MT5 bridge and tunnel must be started on the user's Windows laptop.
- The public dashboard must have a production deployment before it can poll the laptop tunnel continuously.
- A temporary trycloudflare.com URL changes when restarted; a named tunnel is needed for a durable public URL.
